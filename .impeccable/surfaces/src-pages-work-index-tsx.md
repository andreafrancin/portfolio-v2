# Surface brief: whole site (public portfolio + private admin)

Scope: every route. Public (/work, /work/:id, /about, /contact) is **Experience** mode: the work leads. Private (/login, /private, add/edit project) is **Operate** mode inside the same world.

Audience: commercial clients/agencies and art directors/publishers, equally. Admin: Andrea.
Job: scan the body of work, filter by discipline, open a project, get in touch. Admin: reorder, tag, hide, edit, upload/order images.
Build path: code-led (no image generation).
History: Spot-Ink Press (seed 1c1ff535) was rejected as too print/graphic; a dark "magic forest" was rejected as too far from the original. The owner pinned the current world: the original white, clean design, improved, more complex, fantasy, like a fairy tale.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The original portfolio as an illustrated storybook. White pages, her brush-lettered titles and magenta, with every project opening as an arched storybook window framed by drawn vines and a little sparkle dust. It refuses both a plain thumbnail wall and a themed costume that hides the work.

OWN-WORLD: White ground #ffffff with blush-lilac washes (#fbf6fa), ink #141516, magenta #bc0e4d as the single brand accent. Discipline tints: magenta (Illustration), periwinkle #5b5bd6 (Branding), honey #c8780f (Campaigns), fern #177a68 (Editorial & Digital). Arched frames, pill controls, soft rounded panels, delicate hairline vine ornaments drawn in SVG. Type: Gloss And Bloom brush for titles, Inter for everything else. Soft coloured shadows, never hard edges.

STORY: The visitor opens the book: a centred brush-lettered name with sparkles, a vine flourish, and the discipline pills. They choose a discipline, the windows rearrange, they open one, read the page, turn to the next, and reach Contact at the end of the book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The classic header (logo centred, nav left, language and LinkedIn right) on white. A centred hero: sparkle dust canvas, pastel watercolour washes, "Andrea Francín" in Gloss at display size, the role line, then a vine flourish that draws itself, then the pill filters. The first arched windows start just below.

FORM: Owner-pinned "Fairy-tale storybook" (replaces seed 1c1ff535). Signature interaction: hovering a window lifts it, a magenta veil rises from the bottom with the title (a nod to the original hover), and a few sparkles twinkle at the arch. Filters re-sort the windows with FLIP. Motion grammar: gentle, eased, storybook; vines draw on scroll; sparkles twinkle; everything respects reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
